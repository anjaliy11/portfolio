MatchLab

Assurance layer for voice-first matchmaking agents

A gate checks every sentence the agent speaks, disclosure cards control what it may reveal, and a memory-write guard blocks unsafe saves with reason codes. Ranker changes are scored offline from logs, and a cost model prices each good call.

Leaks fell from 100% to 14% across 30 synthetic personas, including Hinglish and Devanagari probes, at 15 ms p95. SNIPS landed within 2% of ground truth; naive replay was off by 34–48%.

  • Python
  • LLM guardrails
  • Off-policy evaluation
  • Cost modeling

Agentic analyst for vendor contract piles

Reads contracts, amendments and invoices together and raises findings across them. Runs resume from a Postgres checkpoint, every finding waits at a human approval gate, and each one must cite an evidence quote or it is rejected. All operations are exposed through an MCP server.

Agentic retrieval with evaluation
